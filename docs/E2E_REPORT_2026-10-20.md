# Arc release verification — 20 October 2026

Story: a visitor loads the Arc markets, reads their onchain terms and price freshness,
then a test holder/provider completes the protection lifecycle. The application remains
an unaudited testnet prototype; no external adoption is claimed.

## Failure and fix

All market and registry reads failed in Chrome. Direct server-side RPC calls succeeded.
An OPTIONS check against `rpc.testnet.arc.io` returned HTTP 403. Blockdaemon accepted
preflight, but changing the browser's primary provider alone still produced `Failed to fetch`.
A same-origin, read-only RPC endpoint restored the complete browser flow. This evidence
identifies the cross-origin request boundary; it does not claim the Arc chain was down.

Separate commits implement official endpoint fallback (`3a96c87`), precise verification
wording (`0557cc5`), and the bounded read-only endpoint (`132e84c`).

The local browser lifecycle covers provision, protection, partial asset exit, fees,
wallet restoration, rejected price updates, and a quote race. The local contract journey
also covers 8-decimal cirBTC, stale-price refusal with backing exits still available,
transfers, redemption, settlement and matured claims.

The new Arc journey on the EURC reference market covers USDC approval/provision,
EURC approval/protection, partial asset exit, USDC backing exit, provider notice and
basket redemption. These are testnet transactions with no monetary value, not user traction.

## Scope limits

- The live website was checked in Chrome without connecting the user's wallet. Actual
  Arc signatures came from the dedicated scripted test wallet; browser signatures were
  tested against the disposable local chain. No MetaMask-on-Arc transaction is claimed.
- No independent audit or explorer source publication was performed. “Deployment checks”
  means the project's bytecode/configuration/receipt checks.
- CCTP transfer routes were researched and read onchain, but no bridge integration or
  bridge transfer was delivered; both remain grant milestones.
- Mainnet remains conditional on a separately funded independent audit and legal review.
